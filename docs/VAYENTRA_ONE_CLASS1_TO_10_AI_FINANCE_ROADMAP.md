# VAYENTRA ONE — Curriculum Roadmap, Class 1 through Class 10
Version: 2026-27 development plan. The Class 1 catalogue is the first committed grade; Grades 2–10 below are original Vayentra PROPOSED enrichment sequences, not claims of completed textbooks or board approval.

## Our curriculum structure
Keep identity and grade independent of subscription. The course hierarchy is:
Board → Grade → Academic year/medium → Subject → Publisher/edition → Chapter → Concept → Original lesson → Practice → Reassessment → Parent progress.

Three labels are mandatory:
1. Official board source mapping: confirmed textbook chapter TITLES only, plus source and edition, no copies of book content.
2. Vayentra school support: original explanations and examples linked to the corresponding chapter. Each lesson has its own review status.
3. Vayentra Original Enrichment: additional AI & Digital Thinking, and Money & Life Skills. Never describe these as NCERT/KTBS mandated subjects in grades where they are not.

## Critical 2026 policy distinction
CBSE has Computational Thinking and AI curriculum resources for Classes 3–8 in session 2026-27 (https://cbseacademic.nic.in/ct-ai.html). Our Class 1–2 AI & Digital Thinking is a play-based, unplugged Vayentra enrichment path; it does not pretend to be the CBSE CT&AI textbook.
India's National Centre for Financial Education includes school resources focused on Classes 6–10 (https://ncfe.org.in/). Our Class 1–5 money activities are original foundational life skills, not a mandated financial subject or paid investment advice.

## School subjects
- CLASS 1 CBSE/NCERT 2026–27: English Mridang (9 chapter titles), Hindi Sarangi (19 chapter titles), Mathematics Joyful Mathematics (13 chapter titles). Sources: CIET official NCERT audio-book chapter indexes.
- CLASS 1 Karnataka State: Kannada, English, Maths, EVS draft subject map. School-specific language order and medium matter; do NOT invent their chapter list. Schools often use selected textbooks, Nali Kali formats or versions, and textbook parts. Confirm with official KTBS school copies in a teacher-led editorial review.
- CBSE school-specific Kannada or third language and optional EVS/Computers should only appear based on the actual school book/board pathway; they are not automatically claimed official NCERT Class 1 core textbooks.
- For every later class, verify real official textbook edition, languages and subject combinations at the time of authoring. Do not assume Classes 2–10 have the same subject list as Class 1.

## Release sequence
A. Grade 1: verify textbook chapter mapping and publish a minimal set of original academically reviewed lessons; test guardian-supervised Class 1 dashboard and practice.
B. Grade 2: repeat a source audit and lesson pipeline using the same schema; carry over Grade 1 prerequisite concepts.
C. Grade 3: integrate formal CBSE CT&AI scope after checking its current edition; ensure child-safe implementation.
D. Grade 4: connect existing Shapes Around Us studio, align original strands with Class 4 scope.
E. Grades 5–8: scalable original lessons, analytics, teacher interventions and gradual digital AI capabilities.
F. Grades 9–10: competency-oriented maths/science/social, language, exam revision and mature future skills. Board-specific academic audit required.
At each grade, do not mark "complete" until ALL assigned subject chapters are mapped to the specific school edition and target outcomes are reviewed.

## Standard original lesson contract for every grade
- Specific grade, board/medium and school-year edition.
- Chapter/subject map with verifiable source reference or explicit "Vayentra original".
- Objective, prerequisite, original age-appropriate explanation, at least one meaningful example or safe activity, misconception, checked questions, correct keys and formative follow-up.
- Teacher review + independent academic review + admin rights check; versioned questions and safe withdrawal history.
- No copied textbook PDFs, unlicensed figures or child images.
- Data-gated access: verified guardian, consent-review evidence, enrolment, approved pilot or entitlement.
- For Classes 1–2, activities should fit about 5–10 minutes with adult-supported reading; do not overburden students with long screen time or written assessments.
